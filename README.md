# Harmony — AI Trust Infrastructure

## Building the trust layer for artificial intelligence.

As AI systems become increasingly powerful, their greatest challenge is no longer capability — it is reliability.

Organizations deploying AI in critical environments face persistent problems:
- Hallucinated outputs
- Lack of verification
- Limited accountability
- Difficulty auditing AI-driven decisions

Harmony is building the missing trust infrastructure layer that enables organizations to deploy AI with confidence.

## What Harmony Does

Harmony provides an AI Verification and Governance Layer between AI systems and end users.

The platform enables:

- AI output verification and validation
- Expert-assisted review workflows
- Human intelligence integration
- Evidence-based reliability assessment
- Auditable AI decision processes
- API infrastructure for trustworthy AI deployment

Harmony transforms AI outputs from unverified predictions into accountable, reviewable, and trusted decisions.

## Initial Focus: Healthcare AI

Healthcare represents one of the most critical areas for AI adoption.

Clinical AI systems can assist with diagnosis, treatment recommendations, and medical decision support — but reliability, transparency, and accountability are essential.

Harmony's initial mission is to help healthcare organizations deploy clinical AI systems with greater confidence through independent verification and governance workflows.

The long-term vision is to expand Harmony's trust infrastructure across all high-stakes industries.

## Vision

AI will become foundational infrastructure.

But intelligence without trust creates risk.

Harmony's mission is to make AI:
- More accurate
- More transparent
- More accountable
- Safer for global adoption
