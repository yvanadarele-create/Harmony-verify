Partner with the verification layer.

Harmony Verify sits underneath other companies' products and stays there. If you build clinical AI, sell into health systems, hold clinical data, or run an evaluation programme that needs expert ground truth, there is probably a shape here that works for both of us.

Shapes that work

Four kinds of partnership.

Type 01

Technology integration

Verification called from inside your product, so your customers see reviewed output without leaving your interface. You keep the relationship; we supply the evidence.

Type 02

Distribution and reseller

You already sell into health systems and your customers are asking the governance question. Verification becomes a line on your proposal rather than an objection to it.

Type 03

Research collaboration

Academic groups and evaluation programmes that need clinician-labelled ground truth — hallucination labels, risk assessments and expert corrections at a scale that is otherwise expensive to produce.

Type 04

Data and dataset licensing

Licensed evaluation datasets built from reviewed, corrected clinical examples — only ever with the originating customer's written agreement.

How applications are assessed

Four requirements, then five scores.

Four things are absolute. Without all four, an application is not assessed at all — the two consent items are not a formality, because partner evaluation involves commercially sensitive information in both directions.

The four requirements

  • A company name
  • A work email address
  • Acceptance of the partnership agreement
  • Acceptance of the confidentiality undertaking

What you get back

A compatibility assessment across five dimensions, each scored out of 100, plus an overall figure and a clear verdict. You see your own scores — we do not publish a ranking or share your assessment with anyone else.

  • Strategic alignment — fit with clinical AI safety
  • Technical fit — API maturity, data formats, infrastructure readiness
  • Business potential — licensing, enterprise and API opportunity
  • Compliance readiness — reputation, regulatory standing, clinical credibility
  • Innovation — how the approach differs from what already exists
Approved PTR-1180
Compatibility assessment Technology integration

Five verdicts are possible: approved, approved with recommendations, under review, not a current fit, and declined.

A high risk assessment declines the application regardless of how well it scores elsewhere. Compatibility does not offset risk — that is the point of scoring them separately.

Apply

Partner application.

Four sections, about ten minutes. Everything here is treated as confidential from the moment you send it.

1 — Company
2 — What you build
3 — What you want from this
4 — Compliance and consent

Do not include protected health information or customer data in this form. If an assessment needs to see clinical material, we will set up a secure channel first.

What happens next

You get an acknowledgement immediately and an assessment within five business days. The assessment names the five compatibility scores and the verdict. If it is a no, it says why — a partnership declined without a reason teaches nobody anything.

What we never do with this

Your application is not shared, not sold, not used as market research, and not shown to another applicant. Commercial assessments are internal, and they stay that way.
