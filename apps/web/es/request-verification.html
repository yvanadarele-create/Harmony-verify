Home / Request verification

Send us the output. Get back a record.

Everything happens on the platform. You submit here, you are told the price and the turnaround before anything starts, and when the review is finished you get an email telling you it is ready. The report itself is downloaded from your account — clinical material does not travel by email.

End to end

What happens after you press submit.

  1. Step 1 · immediate

    Triage and classification

    The submission is read for specialty, case complexity and clinical risk. Nothing is assigned yet and nothing is charged — this only decides what kind of case it is.

  2. Step 2 · immediate

    Price and turnaround, before you commit

    The three classifications produce a price and a turnaround target. You see both, along with the reasoning, and approve before anything proceeds. Accepting creates a payment page for that exact amount and nothing else.

  3. Step 3 · minutes

    Matched to a clinician

    Routing is by specialty first, then availability and rating. Only reviewers who are active, credentialled and have signed the agreement can receive a case — the check runs on both conditions at assignment time.

  4. Step 4 · within the SLA

    Structured clinical review

    Six dimensions: medical accuracy, hallucination, missing information, unsafe recommendations, clinical reasoning and overall risk. Then the corrections, with sources.

  5. Step 5 · on completion

    Email, then download

    You are emailed the moment the verification is ready. The email carries no clinical content — you sign in and download the report as PDF or Word. It opens with a thank you for trusting Harmony with the reliability of your AI outputs, and closes with the reviewer's name, credentials and signature.

What the report contains

Written to be handed to a clinical governance committee without translation — which means no internal jargon, no unexplained scores, and every judgement traceable to the person who made it.

  • Cover page — reference, date, specialty, verdict and overall risk
  • The original AI output, exactly as submitted
  • Assessment against each of the six rubric dimensions
  • Every correction, with the source that supports it
  • A verification score with the deductions itemised
  • Reviewer name, credentials, licence and institution
  • An audit trail: who accessed the case, and when

What it will cost

Specialty, complexity and clinical risk set the price. You can see the number for your own case mix before you submit anything — the simulator runs the same engine that produces the invoice.

No patient identifiers, please

De-identify before sending. Where identifiers do appear, the material is handled as special-category data under your instructions — but the safest identifier is the one that was never transmitted.

Your data stays yours

Submissions are used to perform the review and never to train models. Inclusion in a licensed dataset is opt-in, per submission, and reversible.
